# Synthetic 13-week cash flow inputs

Everything in this folder except this README is generated by the `generador_flujo`
package. All banks, customers, suppliers and amounts are fictitious. A test regenerates
the files and fails if they differ, so never edit them by hand.

```bash
PYTHONPATH=examples python3 -m generador_flujo                       # rewrites this folder
PYTHONPATH=examples python3 -m generador_flujo --salida /tmp/x --semilla 7
```

Exit codes: `0` written, `2` invalid parameters (nothing is written).

## Determinism

- One `random.Random` seeded from `parametros.json -> semilla` (or `--semilla`).
- Amounts are `Decimal` with two decimals; files are UTF-8 without BOM, LF line endings,
  rows sorted by their natural key, no timestamps. Same parameters, same bytes.
- Every count, amount range and planted edge case comes from
  `examples/generador_flujo/parametros.json`; none is hard-coded.

## Files

The cutoff date is `parametros.json -> corte` (default `2026-11-02`).

| File | Columns | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `saldos_bancarios.csv` | cuenta, banco, fecha_saldo, moneda, saldo | One opening balance per account, dated on the cutoff |
| `cxc_antiguedad.csv` | cliente_id, cliente, factura, fecha_factura, fecha_vencimiento, moneda, saldo | Open receivables, plus credit balances (`NC-*`, negative) and zero balances; invoice dates never after the cutoff |
| `cxp_programadas.csv` | proveedor_id, proveedor, factura, fecha_programada, moneda, monto | Supplier payments scheduled from the cutoff on, plus overdue ones dated before it |
| `nomina.csv` | periodo, descripcion, fecha_pago, moneda, monto | Two payroll runs per month (fixed day and month end), moved back to Friday on weekends |
| `impuestos.csv` | impuesto, periodo_fiscal, fecha_limite, moneda, monto | One payment per tax per month for the previous month, moved forward to Monday on weekends |
| `totales_control.json` | | Known answer (`totales_control_flujo/v2`): record count and amount sum per file, and under `casos` the planted special cases (credit balances, zero balances, overdue payments) with their counts and sums |

Payment amounts are unsigned in the files; the flow model applies the cash sign.
Receivable balances are positive except for the planted credit and zero balances.

## Planted edge cases

- **Aging boundaries** (`cxc_antiguedad.antiguedades_borde`): invoices whose days past due
  at the cutoff are exactly 0, 1, 30, 31, 60, 61, 90, 91 and 180, plus invoices not yet due
  that fall due in week 13 (`-85`, `-90`) or after the horizon (`-91`, `-100`).
- **Horizon boundaries** (`cxp_programadas.dias_borde`): supplier payments on the cutoff
  day (day 0), the last and first day of weeks 1 and 2 (days 6 and 7), the last day of the
  horizon (day 90) and the first day after it (day 91).
- Payroll and tax payments are generated for `dias_programacion` days (105 by default),
  so some fall after week 13 and must be reported as outside the horizon, not dropped.
- **Special cases**, drawn after every ordinary record so they do not change them:
  `cxc_antiguedad.saldos_acreedores` receivables with a negative balance (customer advances
  or credit notes, folio `NC-*`), `cxc_antiguedad.saldos_cero` receivables with a zero
  balance, and supplier payments overdue by `cxp_programadas.dias_vencidos` days (default
  1 and 20 days before the cutoff). The flow model must place the credit balances and the
  overdue payments in week 1 and count the zero balances.
